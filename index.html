AFTERSHOCKA Varenna earthquake simulation

Join a cohort

For four days you will live through an earthquake response from two sides: as a family trying to recover, and as part of an organisation trying to help. Decisions take 2–3 minutes and arrive as notifications.

We log your in-game decisions for the debrief and for comparing cohorts. Exports use your name only inside your cohort.
